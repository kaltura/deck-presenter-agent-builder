# Canopy deck outline (fictional demo)

This file stands in for the raw slide deck a real project would start from.
Everything below is invented for this demo. Canopy, Cascade Fieldworks, and
Fernvale Orchards are fictional.

## Slide 1: Canopy: know exactly when and where to water
Water the right spot, at the right time, every time. Field-level soil
moisture. One dashboard for every block. Alerts before stress shows.

## Slide 2: Guesswork costs water and yield
Calendar-based watering guesses in both directions. Overwatering wastes
water and power. Underwatering shows up as yield loss weeks later. Neither
mistake is visible until it's too late to fix.

## Slide 3: What Canopy does
One live picture of every field. Soil moisture sensors. Weather-aware
scheduling. Works with existing irrigation hardware.

## Slide 4: How it works: sensors in the field
Sensors report from the ground, not just the sky. Soil moisture. Soil
temperature. Soil salinity.

## Slide 5: How it works: the data pipeline
Sensor readings, weather feed, combined moisture forecast. Fictional
architecture, invented for this demo.

## Slide 6: The dashboard and alerts
See stress before it becomes damage. Map view by field. Amber-before-red
warnings. Phone alerts for the operations team.

## Slide 7: Automation: closing the loop with valve control
Automatic where you want it, manual where you don't. Connects to existing
valve controllers. Threshold-based automatic watering. Manual override
always available.

## Slide 8: A fictional result: the Fernvale Orchards story
Fictional customer, fictional season, for illustration only. Four blocks
monitored. One season of fictional data. Used to demonstrate a results
slide, not a real claim.

## Slide 9: What it costs
Per Field: 40 per field per month, up to 20 fields. Whole Farm: 600 per
month, unlimited fields, one farm. Pricing is invented for this demo.
