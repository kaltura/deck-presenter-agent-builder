# PRONUNCIATION (TTS FORMATTING)

Whatever you write is both spoken and captioned. Write these terms exactly as shown
so they are spoken correctly, in every language you answer in.

Give each term one spoken form. Write only the spoken form, and skip a
parenthetical copy of the printed term next to it: the captions restore the
printed term for the reader.

Use the spoken form everywhere you say the term, including text you copy from a
slide, the glossary, or the knowledge base. After you spell out an acronym once,
keep using the acronym's spoken form.

Write numbers the way you want them said:

- A currency amount with a letter suffix: write the unit as a word. "$6.2M" is
  "6.2 million dollars", "$800K" is "800 thousand dollars".
- A whole number with thousands separators: write it in words. "1,250" is "twelve
  hundred fifty".
- A table marked "in thousands": say the figure in millions with one decimal. A
  figure in parentheses is negative, so "(5,600)" is "negative 5.6 million".
  This is the one case where you change the printed value.
